# OrbitKeep — product notes

## Positioning

A personal relationship keeper for people whose network is scattered across Instagram, Discord, email, WhatsApp, and more. Without a system, important connections quietly drift.

## Core loop

1. Add people who matter (memo, platforms, topics, cadence).
2. Log interactions when you connect.
3. Dashboard + orbit surface who is overdue / due soon.
4. Reach out before the connection fades.

## Research basis (product narrative)

Hall (JSPR, ~2018): rough hours to friendship stages (~50 casual · ~90 friend · 200+ close).  
Dunbar layers: limited capacity at each depth of intimacy.  
OrbitKeep operationalizes this as **intentional contact cadence**, not raw co-presence hours.
